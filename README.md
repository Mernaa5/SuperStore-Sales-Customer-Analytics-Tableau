# SuperStore Sales & Customer Analytics | Tableau

## Project Overview

https://public.tableau.com/views/SalesDashboardDynamic_17616868599230/SalesDashboard?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link

Developed two interactive Tableau dashboards to analyze SuperStore's sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, and year-over-year growth in 2023 compared with 2022.

The project provides insights into business performance, sales trends, product subcategories, customer purchasing patterns, and customer profitability.

## Tools & Skills

- Tableau
- Data Visualization
- KPI Development
- Sales & Profitability Analysis
- Customer Analytics
- Year-over-Year (YoY) Analysis
- Dashboard Design

## Key Performance Indicators

| KPI | 2023 Value | YoY Growth |
|---|---|---|
| Total Sales | $733K | +20.4% |
| Total Profit | $93K | +14.2% |
| Total Quantity | 12K | +26.8% |
| Total Customers | 693 | +8.6% |
| Sales per Customer | $1,058 | +10.8% |
| Total Orders | 1,687 | +28.3% |

## Dashboard 1: Sales Performance

The Sales Dashboard provides insights into:

- Monthly sales, profit, and quantity trends
- Year-over-year performance comparisons
- Highest and lowest performing months
- Sales and profit by product subcategory
- Sales and profitability trends over time

## Dashboard 2: Customer Analytics

The Customers Dashboard provides insights into:

- Total customers and customer growth
- Sales per customer
- Total orders and order growth
- Customer distribution by number of orders
- Top 10 customers by profit
- Customer purchasing patterns

## Key Business Insights

- Sales increased by 20.4% compared with the previous year, reaching approximately $733K.
- Profit increased by 14.2%, reaching approximately $93K.
- Total orders grew by 28.3%, outpacing customer growth of 8.6%.
- Sales per customer increased by 10.8%.
- The customer distribution shows that many customers placed only one or two orders, highlighting an opportunity to improve repeat purchasing.
- Product profitability varies across subcategories, supporting the need to monitor underperforming products.

## Business Recommendations

- Develop retention initiatives to encourage repeat purchases.
- Investigate low-profit and loss-making product subcategories.
- Monitor monthly sales and profit trends to identify performance changes.
- Analyze high-value customers to support targeted marketing strategies.
- Track customer growth alongside order frequency and sales per customer.
